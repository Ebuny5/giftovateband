Build a production-grade 3D interactive web application for the "Giftovate Band" by Giftovate Therapeutics Ltd.

Adopt the EXACT web layout, styling, and interactive architecture seen in the Puter medical wearables (HydroGlove, HydroSock, and HydroVisage):
- Top half: Interactive Three.js 3D Viewport (~52vh) with studio lighting, soft circular floor shadow, and floating bottom pill toolbar with always-visible labels.
- Bottom half: Segmented 3-tab navigation ("Biometrics", "Design & parts", "Live Telemetry") and scrollable workspace.
- Responsive design: Stacks vertically on mobile; splits into 3D viewer on the left and tabbed workspace on the right on desktop.

---

### 1. Product Identity & Concept
- Product Name: Giftovate Band
- Subtitle: Clinical-grade EDA & HRV biometric wearable · concept v0.1
- Company: Giftovate Therapeutics Ltd
- Core Function: An always-on wrist wearable engineered for hyperhidrosis warriors. It continuously monitors Electrodermal Activity (EDA / skin conductance) and Heart Rate Variability (HRV) from the ventral wrist to detect sympathetic nervous system surges and predict sweat episodes before visible sweating manifests.
- Python Integration: Designed to stream real-time biometric telemetry via a Python backend API / WebSocket directly into the HidroAlly mobile app and trigger proactive cooling alerts.

### 2. 3D Model Specifications (Three.js WebGL Engine)
Build a realistic, procedural 3D wristband model with:
1. Ergonomic Wrist Strap: Breathable, sweat-resistant fluoroelastomer strap with molded micro-ribbing and buckle.
2. Ventral Sensor Pod (Skin Interface): Contoured pod sitting flush against the palmar wrist (highest sudomotor sweat gland density).
3. Dual Ag/AgCl Dry EDA Electrodes: Two circular, gold/silver-plated dry contact electrode studs measuring tonic & phasic skin conductance (0.01 to 20 μS).
4. Optical PPG Sensor Cluster: Central optical window with dual green & infrared micro-LEDs and photodiode photodetector for continuous BPM & HRV.
5. Skin Temperature Thermistor: Precision thermal contact bead to correlate metabolic sweat vs. emotional sympathetic sweat.
6. Main Chassis: Sleek matte-graphite enclosure with surgical teal accent trim.
7. Top OLED Micro-Display: Live digital readout of Heart Rate (BPM), EDA (μS), and sympathetic surge status.
8. Exploded View (Layer Stack):
   - Outer Chassis & OLED Window
   - Multi-layer Rigid-Flex PCB (MCU, Bluetooth Low Energy, AFE)
   - 180mAh Li-Po rechargeable battery
   - Hermetic silicone seal (IP68 water resistant)
   - Dual EDA contact plates & optical PPG sensor lens
9. X-Ray Mode: Outer housing turns translucent smoke-gray, revealing internal copper/silver traces, battery cell, and LED emitter optics.
10. Numbered 3D Screen-Space Hotspots (1 to 8):
   - Hardware-accelerated direct DOM buttons (.hotspot) with numbered badges:
     1. Dual Ag/AgCl Dry EDA Electrodes
     2. Optical PPG Heart Rate / HRV Cluster
     3. Medical Thermistor (Skin Temperature)
     4. Ventral Ergonomic Sensor Pod
     5. Fluoroelastomer Sweat-Proof Wrist Strap
     6. Rigid-Flex MCU & BLE Architecture
     7. 180mAh Li-Po Cell & Magnetic Pogo Charger
     8. Micro-OLED Biometric HUD
   - Hotspots automatically hide when facing away from the camera.
   - Clicking a hotspot opens an inspector card (Title, Description, Engineering Specs, Production Cost).
11. Floating Toolbar with Persistent Labels (Always visible on all screens):
   - [Wrist] (Front view of top display)
   - [Sensors] (Rotates 180° to inspect the skin-contact electrodes on the bottom)
